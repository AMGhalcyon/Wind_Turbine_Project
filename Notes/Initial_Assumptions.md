
# Initial Assumptions



Turbine type - 3 blade horizontal Axis Wind Turbine

Blade Length - 1.2 m

Blade Material - Fiberglass Composite (Simplified isotropic properties

Load - 1000 Pa

Blade Shape - tapered + twisted blade

Loading - Simplified distributed aerodynamic loading OR w(x)=w0(1−x/L)Meaning:highest loading near root, lower toward tip. This roughly mimics realistic blade loading.

Structural Model - cantilever beam

Number of Blades - 3

Rotation Speed - Constant RPM

Flow Type - Steady incompressible flow

Deflection Assumption - Small deflection theory

Failure Consideration - Static stress only initially ( fatigue can come later)





## BOUNDARY CONDITIONS FOR SIMULATION

Physical Reality - Engineering Simplification

Blade attached to hub - Fixed support

Wind acting on blade - distributed load

Aerodynamic pressure - Pressure loading

Blade bending - Deflection in beams

Blade material - Elastic solid



## Insights:

\- Boundary conditions determine simulation realism

\- The root constraint controls structural behaviour massively

\- Distributed loading approximate the aerodynamic force

\- Pressure loading is more physically realistic

\- Simulation is a simplification of reality
