# BEAM GEOMETRY
L - 1.2 m
b or height h - 0.8 m
thickness (t) - 0.4 m
Distributed Load (w) - 100 N/m

# SIMULATING AS A CANTILEVER BEAM

MAX BENDING MOMENT
M (max) = wL^2/2

occurring at the root of the cantilever, the fixed end


MOMENT OF INERTIA

I = bh^3/12

IF thickness increases, the stiffness increases

MAXIMUM BENDING STRESS

stress = M c /I

c = h/2

Maximum stress at root surface

THUS, MAXIMUM STRESS occurs at the root and on the outer surface
should be shown by ANSYS


MAXIMUM TIP DEFLECTION 

DEFLECTION = wl^4/8EI

this can be derived from the singularity function

aluminium or fiberglass, you will have to choose to obtain a value of E

OBSERVATIONS
Maximum stress occurs at blade root
Increasing height strongly improves stiffness
Longer blades dramatically increase deflection
Geometry affects stiffness more than expected
Cantilever behaviour matches that of the turbine blade
